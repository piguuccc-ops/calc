Tip contact test

Press one case button ten times. Every contact the screen registers lands here with its position and footprint. Spread is your print tolerance; misses are your coupling problem.

#x mmy mmcontact ⌀force

Contact ⌀ under about 4 mm is the usual cause of intermittent keys — the digitiser wants a finger-sized footprint. Nothing at all means the aluminium has no conductive path back to your hand.

Screen scale

Android reports a rounded pixel density, so a CSS millimetre is not a real one. Fix that once: stand a bank or ID card on the bar and drag until the bar matches its short edge — ISO ID-1 is 53.98 mm, tighter than any spec sheet.

If you know the display's active width, use screen width — it's exact and beats any card. A Mi 11 Pro is 71.0 mm wide (6.81″, 1440 px at 515 ppi). The card bar is the fallback for an unknown phone.

Alignment only holds in fullscreen — outside it the address bar shifts the viewport top and every key moves with it. Tap ⛶ before fitting the case on.

Case geometry

Your drawing sets every proportion. Keypad span is the one number that isn't in it — pick it, and the rest follows at the ratios measured off your Blender screenshot.

Fit to screen solves for the largest square keys the flat centre allows and gives the window what's left. Everything below is millimetres on the glass — edit any of it and the drawing follows.

Note pages

The Notes key swaps the window to these; ← and → page through them. One page per block, first line is the title. Twenty-seven keys can't type prose, so write the pages here.

Separate pages with a line containing only ---; the first line of each page is its title. An image sits in a page as [img:…] — add one and it lands on a new page you can retitle. Pages sync to the artifact; calibration stays on this phone.
